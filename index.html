Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

A few honest answers about working with a San Diego lawn care and irrigation team.

What areas do you serve?

Rooted Garden Services works on yards throughout San Diego, CA. Call or text to confirm coverage for your specific address before scheduling.

Do you handle both lawn care and irrigation repair?

Yes. Lawn maintenance and irrigation work, sprinkler repair, drip systems, timers, and leak fixes, are all handled in-house, so you're not juggling separate contractors.

My sprinklers are leaking or a zone won't turn on, can that be fixed?

Leak diagnosis and repair for sprinkler and irrigation systems is a core part of the work. Describe what you're seeing when you call, and it can usually be assessed quickly.

Can you install a new irrigation or drip system from scratch?

Yes, new sprinkler and drip irrigation systems are designed and installed for even coverage, along with timer setup so the system runs on its own schedule.

How do I get a quote or schedule service?

Call or text (619) 715-1411 with your address and what's going on with your yard or irrigation system to get started.
